    What to watch

      Methodology & data

      Data

      • Futures, ETFs, equities and option chains: Yahoo Finance (via yfinance).
      • Treasury yields, EIA weekly retail gasoline (GASREGW), BLS average electricity price (APU000072610): FRED.
      • A GitHub Action runs every weekday after the US close, recomputes everything and redeploys this page. Curve snapshots and key metrics are appended to a log in the repo, so the history charts grow over time.

      Assumptions

        Models

        • Carry: annualised roll yield = ln(F1/F12)/ΔT; net convenience yield from the cost-of-carry relation.
        • Implied vol: Black-Scholes inverted on bid/ask mids. American options and ETF dividends are treated as European, which is a known approximation. Term structure is interpolated in total variance σ²T.
        • Outlook bands: lognormal around the futures price, P(FT > K) = N(d2). These are risk-neutral probabilities: they embed risk premia and are not forecasts of physical probabilities.
        • Pass-through: OLS of weekly retail gasoline on weekly-average WTI over 3 years.
        • Factor model: OLS on daily log returns with SPY and front-month WTI as factors. The front-month series has roll gaps, which add noise to βoil.
        • VaR: 95% historical quantile and Expected Shortfall; parametric 1.645·σEWMA.
